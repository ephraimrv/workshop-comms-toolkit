PLEASE INSTALL BEFORE THE SESSION

Each session runs for two hours, and we will begin working with the data straight away. Please complete the installation at home before July 23 -- there will not be time to install software during the session, and campus wifi will be slow with everyone downloading at once.

Step 1. Install R first, then RStudio. The order matters, as RStudio needs R to already be present.

  R:       https://cran.r-project.org/
  RStudio: https://posit.co/download/rstudio-desktop/

Step 2. Open RStudio, and paste the following into the Console panel (bottom left), one line at a time, pressing Enter after each:

  install.packages(c("readr", "dplyr", "ape", "ggplot2"))
  install.packages("BiocManager")
  BiocManager::install("phyloseq")

The last line installs a large package and may take 10 to 20 minutes. If you are asked whether to update other packages, answering "n" (no) is fine. Some red text during installation is normal and does not necessarily mean something has failed.

Step 3. Confirm everything is ready by pasting this in and pressing Enter:

  library(readr); library(dplyr); library(ape); library(ggplot2); library(phyloseq)

If that produces no error message, you are set.

WORKSHOP FILES

Please download the datasets before the session as well:

  https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1YE5dAQdELyjc7rphk2rf9KpaamAcP25e?usp=drive_link

The folder contains the datasets for both parts of Workshop 1, along with a README. The .html files can be opened in any web browser -- just double-click them.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

If the installation fails, or you are unsure whether it worked, please message me by Wednesday evening, July 22, so we can sort it out before the session rather than during it. Screenshots of any error message are very helpful.

3. Certificates for the R Workshop Series — how they will be awarded

Sent 22 July 2026, 11:00 AM · 61 recipient(s) · session: 1
Announcement of the certificate tiers: one Certificate of Attendance per session attended, replaced by a single Certificate of Participation for attendance at all three. Certificates to be released after 6 August 2026.
Good day, everyone,

Ahead of tomorrow's first session, here is how certificates for "Beyond What We See" will be awarded, and one thing worth knowing before you decide which sessions to attend.

CERTIFICATE MECHANICS

• Attend one session — one Certificate of Attendance for that session.
• Attend two sessions — two Certificates of Attendance, one for each.
• Attend all three sessions — one Certificate of Participation covering the complete series.

The Certificate of Participation replaces the individual Attendance certificates rather than adding to them. The distinction is worth understanding. A Certificate of Attendance records that you were present on a particular date. A Certificate of Participation certifies that you completed the program in full; six hours of structured training across three sessions. It is a statement about what you finished, not about where you were on a given morning, and it is the one you would want on a CV or a LinkedIn profile.

If you are currently signed up for two of the three sessions, this is our reason for encouraging you to complete the series. The third session costs two more hours and changes what your certificate says about you.

We should be honest about what a certificate is and is not. Six hours of R will not, by itself, open a door. What it can do is give you a defensible line on a CV and — more usefully — a skill you can actually demonstrate on your own data. The certificate is the receipt. The skill is the asset.

HOW ATTENDANCE IS RECORDED
An attendance sheet will be available at each session. Please sign it on arrival. The sheets are the sole basis for certificate eligibility, so an unsigned sheet means no record, regardless of whether you were in the room.

HOW YOUR NAME WILL APPEAR
After each session you will receive a short evaluation form. It asks for your name exactly as you wish it printed on your certificate, along with your email address. Please write it carefully, including any middle initial, suffix, or accented character. Where a participant submits more than once, the most recent submission will be used.

WHEN CERTIFICATES WILL BE ISSUED
Certificates will be prepared and released after 6 August 2026. They cannot be issued earlier, because the certificate a participant receives depends on how many sessions they ultimately attend, and that is only known once the series has concluded. We will confirm the release date closer to the time.

If you need to change which sessions you are attending, please reply to this email so the roster stays accurate.

We look forward to seeing you tomorrow, 23 July, 9:00–11:00 AM at the Center for Flexible Learning, MMSU Batac.
